# The Long Ride East · art direction

**Idea:** he never stopped riding. Alexander survives in a mosaic, so he is a mosaic cut-out; the world around him is torn paper, so each section tears in over the last.

**Palette (closed):** cream #E6DACB (paper) · light #F9E7D5 (highlights, fibre edges) · terracotta #C5764F (sun, route, flames) · mosaic red #9C5745 (script lines, 329–325 BC) · deep green-grey #2C372D (hills, section 6) · olive #736C51 (hills, 334–333 BC) · umber #5C4139 (horse, shadows) · ink #1F100D (text).
**Banned:** blue, bright green, neon, digital gradients, gloss, photos or realistic faces.

**Type:** Cormorant Garamond 500 headings · Jost 500 spaced capitals for labels and years · Mrs Saint Delafield for the one script line per section, rotated −5°. Self-hosted.

**Technique:** seeded torn edges with a light fibre underlayer and soft shadows; an SVG grain filter plus a page-wide grain overlay; mosaic tiles clipped into each body part, with a rough white cut-out border; 12 fps stepped motion for the figures and the clips.

**Signature move:** the rider loop (8.2 s: gallop to 80%, rear with the sword up, ride off, 1.5 s of empty scene), and his last ride in section 7.

**Devices:** a route map that tears in at the end of section 3, fills in one colour per section, shrinks to the corner (a strip on phones) and is torn apart in section 6. A timeline bar that fills from 356 to 325 BC, takes over the screen in section 6 to squeeze 2,300 years into one move that lands on a "Today" stamp, then shrinks back full.

**Note on the copy:** the text is used word for word from the brief. One fact to check: section 3 says "At 22 he crossed into Asia". He was born in July 356 BC and crossed in spring 334 BC, so he was 21 (most sources say "aged 21" or "22nd year").
